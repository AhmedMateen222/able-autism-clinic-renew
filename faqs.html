Help & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear, compassionate answers about starting therapy, clinic visits, session structures, and our ongoing partnership with families.

1. General & Getting Started

Our philosophy is centered on compassion, respect for neurodiversity, and evidence-informed developmental therapy. We do not attempt to force conformity or "cure" autism; instead, we nurture functional communication, emotional regulation, motor skills, and self-confidence while celebrating who each child is.

We serve children from early toddlerhood (around 18 months to 2 years) through adolescence (up to 16 years). Our Early Intervention pathway is specially tailored for children ages 2 to 5 years.

While having a formal pediatric diagnosis is helpful for comprehensive developmental planning, it is not required for an initial consultation. If you notice developmental delays or sensory differences, you are welcome to schedule an initial evaluation.

Therapy plans are co-created with parents based on baseline observations, sensory assessments, and meaningful real-world goals. We focus on milestones that create real daily improvements in communication and autonomy.

2. Therapy Sessions & Clinic Environment

Our entire facility is designed to prevent sensory overload. We use soft, warm lighting (no harsh buzzing fluorescents), acoustic dampening, cozy calm-down corners, and dedicated sensory gyms featuring swings, crash mats, and tactile equipment.

Individual therapy sessions (Speech or Occupational Therapy) typically range from 45 to 60 minutes. ABA sessions or multidisciplinary intensive programs may range from 2 to 3 hours depending on your child's age, stamina, and therapy plan.

We view dysregulation as an expression of distress or unmet sensory/communication needs. Our therapists respond with calming co-regulation, reducing sensory demands, providing deep pressure or quiet space, and offering unconditional emotional reassurance.

Yes! As a multidisciplinary clinic, our BCBAs, Speech Pathologists, and Occupational Therapists collaborate under one roof, providing cohesive, integrated therapy without conflicting methods.

3. Family Partnership & Home Life

Absolutely. Parents are welcome to join sessions, observe through our observation windows, or participate in structured parent-coaching portions so strategies can be seamlessly mirrored at home.

We host sibling support workshops and provide guidance for parents on balancing family time, helping siblings understand neurodiversity positively, and celebrating every child's unique voice.

We conduct informal verbal debriefs after every session and provide formal comprehensive progress reports every 3 to 6 months to celebrate milestones and adjust goals.

4. Scheduling & Clinic Operations

Able Autism Clinic is open Monday through Friday from 8:30 AM to 5:30 PM. Saturday morning sessions may be available by special appointment for select programs.

You can easily request a consultation using our online appointment form, contacting our clinic phone number at [Clinic Phone Number], or sending us a message on WhatsApp.

Please bring any previous developmental or school evaluations (if available), your child's favorite comfort toy or blanket, and any communication device or sensory items they enjoy.
