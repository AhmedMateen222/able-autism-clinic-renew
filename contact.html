Sensory Considerations

Visiting Able Autism Clinic

We want your child's visit to be as peaceful, welcoming, and anxiety-free as possible.

Convenient Parking

Dedicated, peaceful parking spaces right in front of our clinic entrance with level ground access.

Sensory Tools on Arrival

Noise-dampening ear defenders, fidget toys, and visual timers are freely available in our waiting area.

Quiet Waiting Zone

No loud televisions or overwhelming stimulus—just gentle soft lighting, comfortable beanbags, and soothing books.

Pre-Visit Sensory Tours

Feel free to request a quick, casual walk-through before your child's formal assessment date.

Dedicated to providing child-centered, compassionate developmental therapy and family support. Redefining normal together.
